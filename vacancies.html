Vacancies

Open posts in the ministry and its bodies. Civil service posts are filled by competition under the Civil Service Act.

Open competitions

ME-26/14

Leading specialist, price monitoring

Place of work
Department of Economic Policy and Forecasting, Talas
Grade
Grade 11; salary 4,100 som a month
Applications until
20 November 2026

Organises the weekly observation of prices at the bazaars, checks the records of the observers and prepares the monthly table of the State Anti-Inflation Programme. Higher education in economics or statistics; Iradi and English; Excel. Travel to Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl.

ME-26/11

Tax inspector

Place of work
Nurzhan District Tax Inspectorate
Grade
Grade 9; salary 3,300 som a month
Applications until
27 November 2026

Receives and checks returns, keeps the accounts of taxpayers, takes part in inspections. Higher education in accounting, finance or law; Iradi; Russian is an advantage in Nurzhan.

How to apply

Send a CV, a copy of the diploma and a copy of the identity card to hr@economy.iradgov.org or bring them to the personnel department, Government House, room 230, by the closing date. Short-listed candidates sit a written test and an interview.

Vacancies in all the state bodies of the Zone are also published together on the portal of the Office of the Governor: iradgov.org/vacancies.html
