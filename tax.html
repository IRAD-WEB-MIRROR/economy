Taxes and the State Tax Service

Taxes in the ISEZ are set by the Tax Code of 2009 and collected by the State Tax Service of the Ministry of Economy through the district tax inspectorates. Royalties and export duties on oil, antimony and electricity are not collected by the Service: they are deducted from export proceeds held in escrow, under the Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority.

Head of the State Tax Service: Nadezhda Kovalenko (since 2017).

District tax inspectorates

Each district administration building has a tax inspectorate; in Talas it is in the Government House annex, rooms 40 to 48 (+996-39-2-21-40-40). Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, lunch 12:30 to 13:30. In March and April the Talas and Nurzhan inspectorates also open on Saturdays from 9:00 to 13:00.
