Memorandum with Uzbekistan: what it means for traders

Following the memorandum on trade and border coordination signed with Uzbekistan on 10 February, the ministry will agree with the Customs Service the list of goods for the simplified clearance of small traders' loads at Bazar-Korgon. Until the list is published, the existing rules apply.

Press service of the Ministry of Economy
