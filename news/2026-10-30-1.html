Weekly price observation begins at four bazaars

On Wednesday 28 October observers of the ministry and the Territorial Statistics Office made the first weekly record of the prices of fourteen staple goods at Bazar-i Kalan and Jıldız Market in Talas, the Central Market in Nurzhan and the Lower Town bazaar in Iyık Janyl.

The records will be the basis of the monthly table of prices which the ministry will publish from January 2027 under the State Anti-Inflation Programme.

Press service of the Ministry of Economy
