Register of Firms: extracts tied to the business census

Under Executive Directive 2026/24 on the census of business entities, from 15 October to 30 November an extract from the Register of Firms will be issued only on showing the census slip. Firms that have not been visited by an enumerator may obtain the slip at the Territorial Statistics Office, Jibek Jolu Avenue 58.

Press service of the Ministry of Economy
