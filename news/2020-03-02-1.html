The ministry opens its new website

The Ministry of Economy has opened its new website, prepared with the support of the UNMI trade facilitation programme. Forms, the register offices, the licensing rules and the tax calendar are now in one place, in Iradi, English and Russian. The pages of the old site will be moved over during the year.

Press service of the Ministry of Economy
