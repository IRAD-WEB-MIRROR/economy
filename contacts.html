Written appeals

Written appeals are accepted by post to the general department of the ministry, Government House, Central Square, Talas, by e-mail to info@economy.iradgov.org, or on form OG-1 at the window of the general department. An answer is given within 30 days.

How to get here

Government House is on Central Square; buses 1, 4 and 7 stop at "Central Square". Visitors show an identity document at the entrance.

Address

Government House, Central Square, Talas: the ministry's offices on the second floor (rooms 201–240); the Register of Firms, the Foreign Labour Quota Section, the Tourism Department and the Talas District Tax Inspectorate in the annex, entrance from Tokmok Street.
