Ministry of Economy

The Ministry of Economy of the Irad Special Economic Zone shapes economic policy, keeps the Register of Firms, issues trade licences, sets the quota for foreign workers and directs the State Tax Service, the Territorial Statistics Office, Iradstandart and the Public Procurement Department. It reports to the Governor.

Notices

2026-10-30
Weekly price observation begins at the bazaars

Ahead of the State Anti-Inflation Programme, observers of the ministry and the Statistics Office record the prices of fourteen staple goods every Wednesday at Bazar-i Kalan and Jıldız Market in Talas, the Central Market in Nurzhan and the Lower Town bazaar in Iyık Janyl.

2026-10-26
Register of Firms: extracts only with the census slip until 30 November

Under Executive Directive 2026/24 an extract from the Register of Firms is issued only on showing the slip of the business census. The slip is given by the enumerator or at the Statistics Office.

2026-10-12
Foreign labour quota: the January window

Employers apply for the 2027 quota in the first working week of January (4 to 8 January 2027), in room 15 of the Government House annex.

Registration of firms and sole traders; licences for alcohol, tobacco, fuel and other licensed trades.
