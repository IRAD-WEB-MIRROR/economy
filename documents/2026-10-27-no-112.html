Order of the Minister · 27 October 2026 · No. 112

On the weekly observation of the prices of staple goods

In force

1. From 28 October 2026 the prices of the goods listed in the annex are recorded every Wednesday between 10:00 and 12:00 at three trading places in each of the following markets: Bazar-i Kalan and Jıldız Market (Talas), the Central Market (Nurzhan) and the Lower Town bazaar (Iyık Janyl).

2. Where a good is offered for dollars, the price is converted into som at the official rate of the Bank of Irad on the day of observation.

3. The Department of Economic Policy and Forecasting, with the Territorial Statistics Office, prepares the monthly table by the fifth working day of the following month, from January 2027.

4. Control of this order is entrusted to the First Deputy Minister B. Esenkulov.
