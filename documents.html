Order of the Minister · 27 October 2026 · No. 112

On the weekly observation of the prices of staple goods

In force

Sets the list of fourteen goods, the four markets and the method of observation used for the monthly table of the State Anti-Inflation Programme.

Order of the Minister · 9 October 2026 · No. 97

On the draft quota for the employment of foreign citizens for 2027

Draft; approval in December 2026

Publishes the draft quota of 3,400 work permits by sector and the dates for applications in 2027.

Executive directive · 8 September 2026 · 2026/24

On the census of business entities

In force

Orders the census of business entities to 30 November 2026 and ties extracts from the Register of Firms to the census slip during the census.

Order of the Minister · 6 February 2024 · No. 14

On the fees for registration and extracts from the Register of Firms

In force

Registration: 300 som for a sole trader, 1,500 som for a company or partnership, 600 som for a cooperative; extract 150 som, urgent extract (same day) 450 som.

Act of the Mejlis · 18 December 2015 · No. 15-14

Public Procurement Act (amended 2021)

In force

Open tender, request for quotations up to 2,500,000 som, two-stage tender and single source (art. 21); bids in the state language (art. 17(4)); unreliable suppliers barred for two years (art. 43).

Code · 30 June 2009 · No. 09-02

Tax Code of the ISEZ

In force, as amended

Profit tax 15%, personal income tax 10%, sales tax 12% (no VAT), payroll contributions to the State Pension Fund 15% and 2%; patents for small traders.
